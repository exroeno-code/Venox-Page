# VEXON TECH service architecture

## Goal

Clarify what VEXON TECH does today, what visitors can express interest in, and what remains proposed or idea-stage while preserving the VEXVANO LABS visual system and the site's honest status language.

## Implementation

- Add `/services` as the consolidated service directory.
- Add `/ai-workflow` as the dedicated page for the first active commercial offer: the AI Workflow Audit/Sprint.
- Add status badges and explanatory copy: Available now, Expression of interest, Proposed, and Idea stage.
- Update shared navigation, homepage CTAs, roadmap data, README route documentation, sitemap, and static route manifest.
- Keep contact and WhatsApp as connection channels, not services.
- Do not invent confirmed fees, dates, accreditation, booking links, customer claims, regulatory status, or live products. Use the supplied KES 20,000 AI Workflow Sprint starting price and keep other proposed offerings unpriced.

## Design

- **Selected style:** cinematic venture-studio editorial (probability 0.07).
- **Core principles:** status clarity, practical language, high-signal hierarchy, and trust before hype.
- **Color philosophy:** retain the dark technical canvas and VEXON gold glow; use restrained amber/cyan accents for status and action rather than decorative gradients everywhere.
- **Layout paradigm:** asymmetric editorial sections, dense service index, and wide status panels instead of generic centered marketing cards.
- **Signature elements:** bracket CTAs, mono status labels, and the existing particle/circuit atmosphere.
- **Interaction philosophy:** every action explains where it leads; proposed services link to contact, while the active AI workflow offer has a dedicated detail page.
- **Animation:** reuse existing reveal and particle treatment; no new motion dependency.
- **Typography:** keep Archivo display, Inter Tight body, and JetBrains Mono metadata.
- **Brand essence:** Kenya-based builders who make practical digital work easier, with clear boundaries around unfinished ideas. Personality: useful, candid, builder-led.

## Project structure

- `routes/services.tsx`: all ten service categories and status explanation.
- `routes/ai-workflow.tsx`: active AI Workflow Audit/Sprint landing page, process, use cases, pricing scope, trust, FAQ, and contact CTA.
- `routes/index.tsx`: primary homepage positioning and CTA updates.
- `components/vexon/TopBar.tsx`: navigation entry.
- `routes/roadmap.tsx`: true stage for the active service and ideas.
- `public/manus-routes.json`, `lib/seo.ts`, and `routes/sitemap[.]xml.ts`: route/discoverability updates.

## Boundaries

The AI assistant remains an idea-stage waitlist. Online computer classes remain expressions of interest with unconfirmed details. Trading education remains concept-stage learning content. Online forex trading remains an idea only and is not a broker, platform, account, deposit, execution, signal, money-management, or personalized-advice service. Proposed systems/services are not presented as already delivered.
